# Watchsync enclosure v15

Original design and release documentation by axelaberg91, CC BY-NC 4.0.
Third-party rights and the root licensing notice remain applicable.

## Changes

The bottom plate now displays `axelaberg91.github.io/watchsync/`. Its QR code
opens `https://axelaberg91.github.io/watchsync/`.

The QR footprint, engraving depth and mechanical features retain their existing
dimensions. The upper shell and coil former are unchanged from hardware-v14.

## Printing and verification

Use the [DIY guide](https://axelaberg91.github.io/watchsync/diy.html#print).
The reference setup is a Prusa CORE One with PLA and 0.20 mm layers. Keep the
existing light-to-dark filament change before the Z=0.60 mm layer for the
recessed markings.

Check the new bottom plate in the slicer and scan the QR code on the finished
print with a phone. Digital geometry and QR checks do not establish physical
print quality, fit, strength or device operation. Physical print validation
has not been performed for this revision.

The release includes the editable SCAD model, three STL parts, licensing
notices, a source manifest and SHA-256 checksums. Earlier packages remain
unchanged.
