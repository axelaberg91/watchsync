Quick start

Connect Watchsync to Wi-Fi and send your first time signal.

Before you start

You need an assembled Watchsync with firmware installed, a USB-C power cable and supply, a phone or computer, and a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network with internet access. Your watch must support one of the available radio time signals.

For a new DIY build, follow the USB installation instructions first. This guide starts with an assembled, programmed device.

1. Connect power

Connect the USB-C cable. On a device without a saved network, the status light turns blue when Wi-Fi setup becomes available. If it already knows your network, skip to step 3.

2. Set up Wi-Fi

  1. Open your phone or computer’s Wi-Fi settings and join watchsync. The setup password is watchsync.
  2. If the setup page does not open, visit http://192.168.4.1. Stay connected even if your phone reports that this network has no internet.
  3. Select your home’s 2.4 GHz network and enter its password. Keep the default device hostname watchsync, or choose a unique name if you have multiple devices, then select Connect and save on the Network settings page. For a hidden network, enter its name manually.
  4. The setup network appears after an initial network search. Opening setup reuses that list; choose Refresh networks only when needed. Once connected, setup closes after about 15 seconds. Rejoin your normal Wi-Fi network.

Open the device page while connected to the same local network as Watchsync.

3. Check the device

Under Network settings, check the current IP address and web address. You can also change the hostname here without entering the Wi-Fi password again.

Open http://watchsync.local. If you chose a different hostname, open http://<your-hostname>.local instead. If the name does not resolve, use the device’s IP address from your router.

Under Radio/time settings, check DCF77 · Europe, Central Europe and automatic daylight saving time for a DCF77 watch, including in Sweden. Other watches need their supported signal and a suitable time zone.

Wait for a confirmed internet time update. Transmission begins at a whole-minute boundary when allowed by the current schedule and pause state.

4. Start watch reception

  1. Place the watch directly on Watchsync and keep it still during reception. Range is limited; try turning or moving the watch slightly if reception fails.
  2. Enable automatic radio reception on the watch and leave it on Watchsync overnight. Match the broadcast schedule to your watch’s reception times. You can also start manual reception for an immediate test.
  3. Check that watchsync shows Transmitting. Between scheduled broadcasts, choose Transmit for 1 hour or double-press the physical button. If paused, you can resume automatic transmission.
  4. Allow several complete time messages. Check the watch’s reception indicator and displayed time.

Reception depends on your watch, placement and interference. If it fails, change the watch’s orientation and try again. See reception troubleshooting.

5. Choose everyday operation

Use Always on while finding a reliable position. Then, if desired, choose Daily schedule and match its times to your watch’s reception times. The schedule follows the selected time zone and daylight saving time.
