A simple daily routine

How it works

  1. 01

    Connect power

    Power Watchsync with USB-C.

  2. 02

    Connect to Wi-Fi

    Connect it to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi so it can get the time.

  3. 03

    Sync your watch

    Place your watch on Watchsync overnight and let it synchronize automatically, or start manual reception on the watch.

Place your watch on Watchsync

Range is limited. Place your watch directly on Watchsync overnight with automatic radio reception enabled, and keep it still during synchronization. If reception fails, try turning or moving it slightly.

Tested with Casio Multi Band 6 watches. Watches from other manufacturers that support one of Watchsync’s transmitted time signals are also expected to work when configured for the matching signal.

The story behind it

Why Watchsync?

Watchsync started with a simple problem: I live outside the DCF77 transmitter’s range, so my radio-controlled watches could not synchronize as intended.

I built Watchsync to bring the time signal closer. It gets the time over Wi-Fi and transmits locally to a compatible watch nearby, so I can keep using my watches’ automatic time setting where the usual radio signal does not reach.

Watchsync is a hobby project. Suggestions for improvements are welcome through the contact form.
